REFUND POLICY

At Coffee Kraft, we brew every cup with absolute precision. However, if your experience wasn't perfect, here is our straightforward refund framework:

1. Wrong or Damaged Order

If you receive the wrong item or if it gets spilled/damaged during delivery, please share a picture of the brutalist boxes or items with our helpline. We will instantly process a free replacement or a full refund.

2. Delivery Delays

The true essence of our signature blends and hot brews lies in their freshness. If your order is significantly delayed beyond the 25-minute tracking time and the coffee gets cold, you are fully eligible to file a claim.

3. Cancellation Window

Cancellations can only be made within 2 minutes of placing the order. Once our team starts roasting and grinding the fresh beans for your brew, cancellation or refund will no longer be possible.
